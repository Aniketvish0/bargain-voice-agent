# Demo Script (2–3 minutes)

> Judges score the demonstrated product, not the pitch. This runs the common path live, shows the negotiation, and lands the one number that makes people lean in: **% saved vs. first quote.**

## Setup (before you present)
- 3 cooperative, briefed phone numbers standing in as hotels (teammates or real, consented small businesses). Give each a *base price* to quote so the negotiation has room to move.
- Seed those 3 numbers + the "book a stay" task in the DB.
- Telegram open on the projector. A mini status board (3 call tiles) visible.
- Rehearse turn-taking until it feels human — this is the Voice score.

## The run

**1. The ask (voice, in Hindi-English).**
Send Doot a voice note on Telegram:
> *"Mujhe Jaipur mein is weekend do logon ke liye room chahiye, purane sheher ke paas, ₹4000 se kam. Try to get me the best price."*

Doot (Saarika) transcribes it and (Sarvam-M) replies with the parsed plan: 3 hotels, the questions it'll ask, **target ₹3,500 / walk-away ₹4,000.**
→ Tap **✅ Approve** *(Checkpoint A)*.

**2. Fan-out (the wow).**
Three calls fire **at once**. On the status board, three tiles go live. The callees answer in Hindi; show one noisy line and one code-switched price ("room ka rate hai three thousand nine hundred, sir"). Doot handles both.

**3. Negotiation + competitive leverage (the star).**
Let the audience *hear* it: Hotel B quotes ₹3,600. Doot, still live with Hotel A (who said ₹3,900):
> *"Sir, mujhe paas hi ₹3,600 mil raha hai — ₹3,500 kar do toh main abhi book kar leta hoon."*

Hotel A drops to ₹3,550. Point at the board updating in real time. **This is the moment — call it out:** "no human can play five vendors against each other live."

**4. The human moment (Checkpoint B).**
Hotel C asks: *"Breakfast add karun? +₹500?"* Doot pauses that call ("ek minute please"), and a Telegram message pops up:
> *"Hotel C: add breakfast for +₹500?"* **[Yes] [No]**

Tap **No**. Doot resumes the call seamlessly. The caller never knew.

**5. The deal sheet (Checkpoint C).**
All calls settle. A ranked card lands on Telegram:
> 🥇 **Hotel A — ₹3,550** *(was ₹3,900, saved 9%)* · AC · near old city · 🎧 listen
> 🥈 Hotel B — ₹3,600 *(saved 0%)* · 🎧
> 🥉 Hotel C — ₹4,100 · breakfast declined · 🎧

Tap the 🎧 on Hotel A — play 8 seconds of the actual negotiation. (Provable, not a claim.)
→ Tap **✅ Book Hotel A**.

**6. Close.**
Doot calls Hotel A back, confirms, captures **confirmation #A4821**, writes it to the DB, and sends the receipt on Telegram.

> **Closing line:** "Five minutes ago I sent one voice note. I made zero calls, negotiated three hotels against each other, and booked the cheapest one — with a recording that proves the price. That's Doot."

## What to have ready for Q&A
- The `savings_pct` across the 3 calls (your headline metric).
- The signed transcript for the booked call (trust).
- Honest scope: payment/ID stay human (Checkpoint C) — a feature, not a gap.

## Fallback if a live call flakes
Have one **pre-recorded** end-to-end run ready to play. Never debug on stage — narrate the recorded run with the same energy.
